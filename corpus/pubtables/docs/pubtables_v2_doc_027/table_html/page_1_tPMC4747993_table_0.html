Dis-adopters/non-adoptersAbandoned the SWC measures and/or never used SWC measures on any of their plots
Initial adoptersEstablished SWC line interventions on up to 25 % of sloping farm land (experimentation phase) and did not yet expand them to other plots
Actual adoptersEstablished and maintained the initial SWC measures during past 4 years, and started to expand them on at least 26–50 % of the vulnerable farm land
Final adoptersContinued use, expanded, and more than 5 years maintained on their own motivation, and in total covering 51–100 % of sloping farm area
